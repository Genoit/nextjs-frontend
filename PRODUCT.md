# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

E-commerce entrepreneurs, dropshippers, small brands without a video team, and agencies or freelancers producing ads for clients. They are mostly non-technical. They arrive with a product link or with product details typed in, and they want a usable video ad without learning video production.

## Product Purpose

TrendED turns an e-commerce product into a usable video ad. It first understands the product, then builds a creative strategy, generates and checks each shot, and assembles the final video. Success is measured as cost per accepted ad.

## Positioning

TrendED is not a prompt-to-video generator. It builds the prompt from a structured, validated product sheet that keeps facts apart from hypotheses. It plans the scenes, routes each shot to a suitable engine, and checks quality per shot before any assembly.

## Operating Context

Flow: paste product URL (or manual input) → product intelligence → creative config, strategy and angles → storyboard → asset preparation → per-shot video generation with accept/rerender → finishing (music, voice, subtitles, export per aspect ratio).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real data available to the UI: the user's products (title, images, price, platform, extraction status) and the video jobs, shots and exports for each product, with estimated and actual costs.
- No store connection (Shopify/WooCommerce), sales, revenue or ROAS data exists. Never show it.
- UI language: English.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: TrendED. Logo: `public/dashboard/trended-logo.png`.
- Brand colours (charter V2): orange #c2410c for actions and CTAs, gold #eab308 used sparingly, success #16a34a, danger #dc2626.
- Typefaces: Inter (UI), Instrument Serif (editorial accents).

## Evidence on Hand

No customers, testimonials, press or performance statistics exist. Do not fabricate them. Mocks must be labelled as mocks, never presented as real generations.

## Product Principles

- Product before prompt: facts and hypotheses are always distinguished.
- No fake success: an API response is not an accepted shot, and a demo figure is not a metric.
- Cost is visible: every generation shows its real or estimated cost.
- The next step is always one click away.
